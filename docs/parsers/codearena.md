# CodeArena

← [Parsers](../parsers.md)

The public [API](https://codearena.uz/api/v1/) allows 120 requests/minute/IP.

Marathons can have a personal time limit within a longer availability window.
Solve times are relative to each participant's personal start.

Marathon standings can remain hidden until the end; an empty hidden table does
not mean there are no participants. Ratings may be published after results.

Accepted CF solutions remain solved despite reduced or zero points.

Usernames are case-sensitive.
